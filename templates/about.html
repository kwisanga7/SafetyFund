About SafetyFund

Who Are We?

In 2026, safety professionals from Kigali Safety Academy generated the idea of working together to save for the future. As the idea continued to grow, more people joined, and that is how SafetyFund was founded.

The initiative was strongly supported by Fredson NIYONIRINGIYE, CEO of Kigali Safety Academy and the current Chairperson of SafetyFund.

Together with the team, policies, a clear vision, a mission, and organizational goals were established. SafetyFund continues to look forward to growing with more members and creating a stronger financial future together.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

“Our mission is to empower safety professionals to save, invest, and share responsibly, creating a stronger and more secure financial future for every member.”

What We Aim To Achieve

Our Objectives

SafetyFund aims to build a financially responsible, supportive and sustainable community.

01

Promote Saving

Promote a culture of saving among safety professionals.

02

Prepare for the Future

Help members prepare for the future by turning regular contributions into meaningful financial resources.

03

Collective Investment

Create opportunities for collective investment through the purchase of shares and pooled funds.

04

Financial Support

Provide responsible access to financial support, including member loans where applicable.

05

Financial Discipline

Encourage financial discipline and accountability through transparent management of members' contributions.

06

Strong Community

Build a strong community of safety professionals who support one another financially.

07

Long-Term Wealth

Create long-term wealth and financial security for members and their families.
